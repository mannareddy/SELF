The RRRR Protocol has two versions depending on what you are facing. Both start with the same first step — Recognize. That moment of noticing is where everything changes.

Version 1 — Stop the Impulse

Recognize → Resist → Return → Release

Use this when you need to stop impulsive behaviour before it happens — revenge trading, sending the angry message, reaching for the drink, reacting before you think. Recognize the trigger. Resist acting on the first impulse. Return to baseline (breathe, step away). Release the charge without acting on it. The behaviour never happens. This is the emergency protocol.

Version 2 — Process the Emotion

Recognize → Reflect → Reframe → Respond

Use this for emotional intelligence and deeper processing — navigating a difficult relationship, a career decision, grief, or a long-held pattern. Stopping the impulse isn’t enough here. You need to understand it. Recognize what triggered you. Reflect on what it is really activating underneath. Reframe the narrative — what is actually true? Then Respond from awareness, not reflex.

The 3-Step Circuit Breaker is the emergency interrupt for the autopilot. When the RRRR Protocol feels too structured for a moment of acute distress, this severs the automatic programme instantly.

The 3 Steps

1. Pause — Box Breathing

Inhale 4 seconds. Hold 4 seconds. Exhale 4 seconds. Hold 4 seconds. One complete box. This severs the neurochemical loop of the auto-pilot programme — no willpower required, just the breath.

2. Witness and Label

Step back. Ask: “What is the exact trigger causing this distress?” Naming the emotion strips its hidden power. You move from being inside the reaction to observing it from the screen.

3. Detached Action

Shift focus to what you can control. Act while remaining detached from the outcome — success or failure. Stop watering the weeds. Act from the SELF, not from the wound.
